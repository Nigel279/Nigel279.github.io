The Mystery Black Box Investigation

In New Media and Tech, we were challenged with figuring out what is inside a mystery black box. The black box is designed so that when water is poured into it, it may release more water, less water, or sometimes no water at all. Our goal was to determine the function of the system inside the box based on evidence and repeated trials.

Black Box Setup

Model and Investigation

In this investigation, we explored a mystery black box that released different amounts of water depending on how much was poured into it. Our goal was to figure out what was happening inside without opening the box. We created a model to try and recreate the system, and we believed it may have used a water wheel or similar mechanism to control the flow of water. We built and tested this model, but it did not work as expected and did not match the results of the real black box. This showed us that the actual system inside is more complex than a simple water wheel and uses a hidden mechanism to control how water is stored and released.

Hypothesis

Based on my initial observations, I believed that the box contained a system of 2–3 tubes and a tipping bucket mechanism. I also thought there may be a water wheel or similar device inside that controls how water is released depending on the amount added.

Observations and Data

During our trials, we noticed that the amount of water coming out was not always equal to the amount going in. In some cases, no water came out at all.

Conclusion

Based on the data, I concluded that the black box likely uses a weight-based tipping system that controls how water is released.
